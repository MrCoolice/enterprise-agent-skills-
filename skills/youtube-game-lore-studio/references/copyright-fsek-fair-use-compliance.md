# Copyright, FSEK m. 35 & YouTube Fair Use Compliance

## 1. Turkish Copyright Law (5846 Sayılı FSEK m. 35 - İktibas Serbestisi)
Under Turkish law, using copyrighted gameplay/cinematics in video essays is governed by FSEK Article 35 (İktibas Serbestisi):
1. **Disclosed Work (Alenileşmiş Eser):** The game or cinematic must be legally and publicly released.
2. **Proportionality (Maksadın Haklı Göstereceği Nispet):** Alıntı strictly limited to illustrating and analyzing the narrative; never replacing the original work.
3. **Independent Transformative Work:** The commentary, voiceover, and historical/philosophical critique must constitute the dominant essence.
4. **Attribution (FSEK m. 37):** Right holders (studios/publishers) must be explicitly credited in the video description and credits.

## 2. YouTube Content ID & US Fair Use (Section 107)
- **Audio/Music Rule:** Never use raw, commercial OST albums. Use royalty-free dark ambient tracks or original acoustic re-scores. Content ID triggers primarily on music.
- **Visual Editing Rule:** Never exceed 8–15 continuous seconds of raw cinematic footage. Intercut with Blender 2.5D parallax, parchment maps, particle overlays, and dark silence pauses.
- **Studio Policies:** Align with Blizzard Video Policy, Games Workshop IP guidelines, CD Projekt RED content guidelines (all grant non-commercial video essay permission with original commentary).
